How to teach a cell

  1. Put a block on the cell's dashed outline.
  2. Hold the arm, then press Free arm. With the torque off, the arm drops if you let go.
  3. Hover: put the open jaws centred above the block, high enough to carry a block over the others. Press Lock and save.
  4. Grab: press Free arm, lower the jaws around the block to the height you want to grip, then Lock and save.
  5. Press Test cell to watch the robot go hover, grab, hover.
